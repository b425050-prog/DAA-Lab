Binary search    | FOUND      | 3        | 5               | 3       
Ternary search   | FOUND      | 3        | 5               | 2       

Asymptotic analysis:
  Binary : T(n) = T(n/2) + O(1) = O(log_2 n)
  Ternary: T(n) = T(n/3) + O(1) = O(log_3 n)

Why binary is normally better here:
  A binary-search level probes one middle element.
  A ternary-search level may probe two middle elements.
  Worst-case probe constants are approximately
      (1/ln 2) ln n = 1.4427 ln n     [binary]
      (2/ln 3) ln n = 1.8205 ln n     [ternary]
  Therefore both are logarithmic, but binary uses fewer array probes
  in the standard comparison model for sorted arrays.
